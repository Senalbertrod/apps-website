Make Forever QR Codes Support

Answers to common questions, and how to get help.

Common questions

Will my QR codes stop working one day?

No. The app only makes codes where the information is inside the code itself, so they don't depend on any server or subscription.

My code won't scan.

Try printing or showing it bigger, and use dark squares on a light background. The app warns you when your colors are hard to scan.

I don't see the Apple Watch button.

The watch button only shows when an Apple Watch is paired with your iPhone and the watch app is installed.

Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Closed, it looks like the iPhone version. Open, every screen uses the big screen, and half-closed like a book, the screens split on the fold. Opening or closing it keeps your place.

Can I get my saved codes back after deleting the app?

No. Your codes are only stored on your device, so deleting the app deletes them. Share or save the important ones as pictures first.

Still need help?

Send an email and describe what happened. If you can, include your device and its software version.
