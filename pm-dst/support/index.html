Common questions

How do I add it to my watch face?

Touch and hold your watch face, tap Edit, swipe to Complications and tap a slot. Under PM & DST, choose AM / PM (then a style) or DST Status.

The DST complication is blank.

That's on purpose: it stays blank during standard time and shows DST only while Daylight Saving Time is on where you are.

Does it work when I travel?

Yes. It follows your watch's time zone automatically, so it stays correct in other countries and when the clocks change.

Still need help?

Send an email and describe what happened. If you can, include your device and its software version.
