PM & DST Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

In short: PM & DST doesn't collect any data. It only reads the time on your watch to show AM, PM and Daylight Saving Time.

What we collect

Nothing. PM & DST has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives any of your information.

What the app uses

  • Your watch's time and time zone: used on your watch to show AM or PM and whether Daylight Saving Time is on. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • PM & DST doesn't ask for any permissions and doesn't connect to the internet.

What stays on your watch

  • The style you pick for the complication is saved only on your Apple Watch.
  • Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

PM & DST doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Sharing

Because no information is collected, nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@senalbert.com.
