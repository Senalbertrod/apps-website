What it does

AM / PM

Four styles to choose from: PM, P.M., pm or p.m.

DST Status

Shows DST while Daylight Saving Time is on, and stays blank during standard time.

Right on the hour

Flips at exactly the right moment, with very little battery use.

Travel ready

Follows your watch's time zone, including when Daylight Saving Time starts or ends.

Live clock

The app shows a ticking clock with a Daylight Saving ON / OFF light.

Apple Watch with watchOS 27 or later. No iPhone app needed.
