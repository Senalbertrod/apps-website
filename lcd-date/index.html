What it does

Day name on its own

THURSDAY or THU in its own complication, in the same retro lettering.

24 date styles

Month first or day first, with slashes, dashes or dots, with or without leading zeros.

Day name, with or without the year

Show THU, 10/01 and 2026 together in one small round complication, or THURSDAY 10/01.

Every complication slot

Circular, rectangular, corner and inline.

Always on time

Flips at your local midnight, even right after you fly to another time zone.

Retro LCD look

Green digital digits that follow your watch face's colors when the face is tinted.

Apple Watch with watchOS 27 or later. No iPhone app needed.
