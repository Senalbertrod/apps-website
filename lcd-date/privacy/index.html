LCD Date Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

In short: LCD Date doesn't collect any data. It only reads the date and time on your watch to show them.

What we collect

Nothing. LCD Date has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives any of your information.

What the app uses

  • Your watch's date, time zone and language: used on your watch to show today's date and day name. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • LCD Date doesn't ask for any permissions and doesn't connect to the internet.

What stays on your watch

  • The style you pick (for example, month first) is saved only on your Apple Watch.
  • Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

LCD Date doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Sharing

Because no information is collected, nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@senalbert.com.
