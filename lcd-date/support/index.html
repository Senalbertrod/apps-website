LCD Date Support

Answers to common questions, and how to get help.

Common questions

How do I add the date to my watch face?

Touch and hold your watch face, tap Edit, swipe to Complications and tap a slot. Choose LCD Date, then Month first or Day first, and pick a style.

I traveled and the date looks wrong.

LCD Date checks the time zone every 15 minutes, so it fixes itself within a few minutes after your watch changes time zone.

The day name is in a different language.

Day names follow your watch's language. Change it in the Apple Watch settings to change the day names.

Still need help?

Send an email and describe what happened. If you can, include your device and its software version.
