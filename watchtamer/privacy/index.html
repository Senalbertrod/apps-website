WatchTamer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

In short: WatchTamer doesn't collect any data. Your dinosaur, your settings and your step count stay on your Apple Watch.

What we collect

Nothing. WatchTamer has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives any of your information.

What the app uses

  • Motion & Fitness (step count): if you allow it, WatchTamer reads your step count on your watch so walking can feed and train your dinosaur. The steps are only used inside the game and are never sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the watch reminds you when your dinosaur needs care. These reminders are made on your watch; nothing is sent to a server.
  • Battle codes: a battle code is a short code that describes your dinosaur for a friend battle. You choose who you share it with, and it never goes through the internet.

What stays on your watch

  • Your dinosaur, game progress, settings and the list of friend codes you've battled are saved only on your Apple Watch.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Children

WatchTamer doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Sharing

Because no information is collected, nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
