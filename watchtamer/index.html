WatchTamer

A 90s-style virtual pet dinosaur for your Apple Watch.

Coming soon to the App Store
  • Raise a dinosaurHatch an egg, feed it, clean up after it, and watch it grow through every stage.
  • Walk to feed and trainJust walk: every 250 steps feeds your dino, every 500 gives protein, and every 1,000 is a training, even while the app is closed.
  • Battle friendsSwap short battle codes and both watches play the same fight. No internet needed.
  • Rare eggsEvery new egg rolls the dice. The rarest ones hatch into a T-Rex or a Raptor.
  • Gentle remindersOptional notifications when your dino is hungry, weak, or needs cleaning.

Apple Watch with watchOS 26.4 or later. No iPhone app needed.
