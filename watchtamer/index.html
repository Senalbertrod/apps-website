WatchTamer

A 90s-style virtual pet dinosaur for your Apple Watch.

Apple Watch

Pictures

What it does

Raise a dinosaur

Hatch an egg, feed it, clean up after it, and watch it grow through every stage.

Walk to feed and train

Just walk: every 250 steps feeds your dino, every 500 gives protein, and every 1,000 is a training, even while the app is closed.

Training reserves

Every 5,000 steps in one day saves a training for later, up to 4. Use one any time from RESERVE on the TRAIN menu.

Battle friends

Swap short battle codes and both watches play the same fight. No internet needed.

Rare eggs

Every new egg rolls the dice. The rarest ones hatch into a T-Rex or a Raptor.

Gentle reminders

Optional notifications when your dino is hungry, weak, or needs cleaning.

On your watch face

A complication shows what your dino needs most (medicine, food, a flush or protein), or your dino when all is well. It stays up to date even while the app is closed.

In English and Spanish

WatchTamer follows your Apple Watch's language.

Apple Watch with watchOS 26.4 or later. No iPhone app needed.
