Domino 4 Player Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short: Domino 4 Player doesn't collect any data. Your players, teams, scores and results stay on your iPhone or iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. Domino 4 Player has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives any of your information.

What the app uses

  • Domino 4 Player doesn't ask for any permissions and doesn't connect to the internet.
  • The practice game's computer players run on your iPhone or iPad.

What stays on your device

  • Your players, teams, the line of teams, scores, results, practice games and settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Children

Domino 4 Player doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Sharing

Because no information is collected, nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@senalbert.com.
