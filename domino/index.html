Domino 4 Player

Keep score on domino night, and practice Cuban dominoes against the computer.

Coming soon to the App Store
  • Quick GameTwo teams of two: enter each round's points, see every round, and fix or undo the last one. The scoreboard keeps count of games won.
  • Teams: winner staysAdd all the teams and the line takes care of itself: the winners stay, the next team comes in, and the losers go to the back of the line.
  • Play to any targetChoose the points to win in steps of 50.
  • ResultsLongest streak, fastest win and biggest round, plus the list of games played.
  • Practice Cuban dominoesDouble-nine, with tranque rules: you and a computer partner against two computer rivals. Make each one Easy, Medium or Hard.
  • Learn modeOn your turn, a card suggests a good move and explains why.
  • Feels like real tilesTap a tile to lift it, flick it up to play it, and drag to arrange your hand. Before each round, tap Shake.
  • Your wayLight or dark mode, and play counter-clockwise or clockwise.
  • In English and SpanishDomino 4 Player follows your iPhone or iPad's language.

iPhone, iPhone Duo or iPad with iOS 27 or later. Free. No account, no ads, no tracking.
