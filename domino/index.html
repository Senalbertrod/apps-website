What it does

Quick Game

Two teams of two: enter each round's points, see every round, and fix or undo the last one. The scoreboard keeps count of games won.

Teams: winner stays

Add all the teams and the line takes care of itself: the winners stay, the next team comes in, and the losers go to the back of the line.

Play to any target

Choose the points to win in steps of 50.

Results

Longest streak, fastest win and biggest round, plus the list of games played.

Practice Cuban dominoes

Double-nine, with tranque rules: you and a computer partner against two computer rivals. Make each one Easy, Medium or Hard.

Learn mode

On your turn, a card suggests a good move and explains why.

Feels like real tiles

Tap a tile to lift it, flick it up to play it, and drag to arrange your hand. Before each round, tap Shake.

Your way

Light or dark mode, and play counter-clockwise or clockwise.

In English and Spanish

Domino 4 Player follows your iPhone or iPad's language.
