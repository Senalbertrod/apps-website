Domino 4 Player Support

Answers to common questions, and how to get help.

Common questions

How does the line of teams work?

In Score, choose Teams. Add all the teams and pick the first two; the rest are shuffled once. After each game, bring in the next team (the losers go to the back), play again with the same two teams, or skip a team that isn't ready. They keep their place in line.

Can I change a player's name during a game?

Yes. Tap the pencil next to the names. The game and the scoreboard stay just as they are.

How do I play a tile in practice?

Tap a tile to lift it, then flick it up to play it. If it fits on both ends, the two places glow on the table: tap where it goes, or tap Put back. You can also drag a tile slowly all the way to a glowing end.

How do I put my tiles in order?

Press and hold a tile until your tiles wiggle, drag them into any order, then tap Done.

I don't hear the shake or feel the vibration.

Sound follows your iPhone or iPad's silent mode and volume, and the vibration on iPhone follows Sounds & Haptics in your iPhone's Settings.

Are my games saved?

Yes. Everything is saved right away on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes it.

Still need help?

Send an email and describe what happened. If you can, include your device and its software version.
